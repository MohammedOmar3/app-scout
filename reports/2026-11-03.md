## Ideas

1. **[Fifty Hours (working name)](../briefs/2026-11-03-fifty-hours.md)** — score 3.7 — RoadReady, the DMV-recommended US teen driving log (★2.9, 19k ratings), was rewritten in June 2026 and now logs families out, loses hours and shows ads. The fix is a no-account log that opens straight to Start Drive, can't lose a drive, and exports a signed PDF.

Score: evidence 4 · demand 3.5 · willingness to pay 2.75 · gap clarity 4.5 · competition 3 · build fit 4.5 → **3.69**.

**How it cleared the gates.** Gate A: 87 relevant low-star reviews in 90 days, rising monthly since June, with the latest on 10-30. The app is US-only, so spread comes from other apps: RoadReady Ohio (6 in 90 days, 25 since 2025-11) and Aceable's driving log (2). RoadReady Ohio is the same vendor, so the independent second app is thin. Gate B: 5 screens, no native extensions, local-first. Kill check: no log app has thousands of ratings; the largest other than RoadReady's own has 159 and was last updated in 2020.

**Weak points, stated plainly:** about 15 small log apps exist and several already target RoadReady's users (Moda, LaneUp, Dryves), willingness to pay is indirect, and DMV referrals drive RoadReady's distribution. The brief lists these. Read them before committing the two weeks.
