## Notes

- `--term-storefronts` replaces us,gb for the term searches rather than adding to them. Put `us,gb` in the list when you want them.
- A term-storefront run stores each app's rating count from that storefront (Structured shows 10k or 30k, not its 169k in us), so check demand on the us count.
- The competitor floor (`target_competitor_min_ratings`, 300) uses the local rating count, so small markets queue few competitors (round 1 queued 13). Consider a lower floor for term-storefront runs.
