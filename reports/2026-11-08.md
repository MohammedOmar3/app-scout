## Ideas

1. **[Plain Kcal (working name)](../briefs/2026-11-08-plain-kcal.md)** — score 3.9. Yazio is top-grossing in 11 European storefronts and has 289 low-star reviews in 90 days. 140 of them are about an ad after every logged food, a long questionnaire (again after a few days away) and popup streaks and animations even paying users can't turn off. They come from de, fr, it, at, nl and four more storefronts, 82 in the last 30 days. The fix: a calorie diary that opens straight to today, with no questionnaire, ads or gamification. It works offline on bundled national food tables (BLS 4.0 is CC BY 4.0; CIQUAL is Licence Ouverte), with Open Food Facts for barcodes, and has one `pro` tier for saved meals, history, export and sync. **Competition:** Fddb's German free tier has no ads, and MyNetDiary has no ads or account but uses US data. The wedge is clear but not unique, and France may be the easier first market. **Kill test on day 2:** BLS/CIQUAL search must return sensible top-3 matches for 20 common foods.
