## Focus segment: islamic (first day)

- 12 of the 12 apps scanned this run came from the segment (40 reserved slots; the run hit its time budget at 12). 13 segment apps now have recent low-star reviews.
- **The pain is real and large:** ads in worship apps are the biggest cross-app theme in the whole digest (8 apps, 101 English keyword hits; ≈175 low-star reviews when Arabic is read too, us, sa, ae, gb, pk, my, au, ca). Reviewers are explicit: immodest ads beside Quran text, music in ads at Fajr, ads before the prayer time loads, paying subscribers still seeing ads.
- **But it is already solved:** the switch target exists and is free (Quran.com, iQuran, Pillars, MAWAQIT). The market problem is discovery, not a missing app. That makes it a poor fit for "fix a known pain" and a high 4.3 risk.
- **What would make a segment idea work later:** a specific *missing feature* or *locale gap* that the ad-free apps don't cover (e.g. a particular madhab or calculation-method complaint, a language without a good free app, a Ramadan-specific workflow), seen across ≥ 2 apps. Ramadan 2027 is projected to start around **8 February 2027** ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/09/02/expected-ramadan-2027-start-date-and-eid-al-fitr-holiday-dates-in-uae/)), so anything found before mid-January still has a "why now" window.
