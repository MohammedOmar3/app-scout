Incumbents are large (Muslim App 88k, Athan Pro 82k, Quran Majeed 241k ratings). **Kill:** these apps already give everything away free with no ads: MAWAQIT ("100% free and without any ads, no subscription", ★4.9, 6.3k, non-profit), Pray Watch ("Always free. And no ads… ever", ★4.8, 9.3k), IslamApp ("full, unlimited access to all features completely free", ★4.9, 6.0k), Quran.com (★4.9, 33.9k) and iQuran ("NO adverts. NO subscriptions", ★4.8, 30.9k). Pillars (★4.8, 5.6k) has no ads but does have a paid tier ([fiveprayer.app](https://www.fiveprayer.app/alternatives/pillars)). → rejected.

**4 · `paywall:athan-pro:lifetime-not-honored`** — People who bought a one-time or lifetime unlock are asked to pay again: "I paid for a premium account… that offers lifetime access, but it only worked for 1 year" (Athan Pro gb 2026-10-28 ★1); "I already paid for lifetime subscription, why you're asking me again" (Athan Pro ca 2026-08-24 ★2); three Saudi lifetime subscribers say the app stopped working for them (sa 09-13, 09-24, 10-08); "I purchased lifetime membership, I change my phone now they asking me for purchase again" (Quran Majeed us 2026-09-13 ★2). Athan Pro's store listing has ten in-app purchases from $3.99 weekly to $99.99 ([apppricinglab](https://apppricinglab.com/iap/apple/743843090)). SCOUT.md counts refunds and support disputes as context, not as an opportunity on their own. The idea that would follow ("a one-time-purchase prayer app") already exists: iPray ($1.99, "NO subscription", ★4.8, 9.1k), and the free apps above. → rejected.

**5 · `bugs:athan-pro:adhan-unreliable`** — "Since the recent app 'make over' the notifications been barely working and I have missed multiple prayers" (Athan Pro us 2026-10-13 ★2); "You hear Athan at sunrise and not at prayer time" (gb 2026-10-23 ★1); "prayer times keep changing with no accuracy" (sa 2026-10-08 ★1, translated); Muslim Pro "says Athaan already happened but did not alert me" (gb 2026-10-12 ★1) and "location won't update itself" (us 2026-10-25 ★1). It started with Athan Pro 9.0 and iOS 27 (Sept 2026), so it's a regression the incumbent can fix, not a gap in the market. Accurate, reliable alternatives exist (MAWAQIT uses each mosque's own times; Pray Watch; iPray). For any future prayer idea: the adhan-js library, a selectable calculation method/madhab, and rescheduling notifications on a rolling window under iOS's limit of 64 pending notifications are exactly what these reviews show going wrong. → rejected.

**6 · `removed:quran-readers:bookmarks-tafsir`** — "After the update… it is starting from the first surah again" (Quran Majeed us 2026-10-23 ★1); "lost all bookmarks, reading mark, notes" (Quran Majeed gb 2026-09-03 ★2); Quran Pro 6.0's redesign ("scrolling is jittery… screen fully lit by default", gb 2026-09-29 ★2; "fix the ability to scroll and listen at the same time", gb 2026-10-09 ★1); Ayah removed Ibn Kathir and al-Sa'di tafsir and al-Sa'di word meanings (us 2026-08-25, sa 2026-09-28; Ayah hasn't been updated since 2025-03). Ten reviews over four apps, gone in a release or two. Quran.com and iQuran already do the job here: bookmarks, notes, scrolling with audio, and several tafsirs, all free. Tafsir and translations also carry their own licences (SCOUT.md islamic check). → rejected.

### What would make a segment idea work later

A specific *missing feature* or *locale gap* that the free ad-free apps don't cover, seen in ≥ 2 apps. For example: a calculation-method or high-latitude complaint (the Nordics, the UK in summer), a language with no good free app (today's reviews came from us, gb, ae, sa, my, pk, ca and a few Gulf storefronts; none from tr, id, bd, fr, de), or a Ramadan-specific workflow (suhoor/iftar timetable sharing, a khatmah planner across a family). Collector tuning that would help: make sure the segment's review scans reach tr, id, bd, eg, ma, dz, fr and de, and rescan Quran.com, Pillars, MAWAQIT and Pray Watch themselves to see what *their* unhappy users ask for. Ramadan 2027 is projected to start around **8 February 2027** ([The National](https://www.thenationalnews.com/news/uae/2026/09/02/expected-ramadan-2027-start-date-and-eid-al-fitr-holiday-dates-in-uae/)); an idea found before about 10 January still gets the "why now" bonus.
