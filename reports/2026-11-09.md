## Notes

- **The same failure, three times today:** a big incumbent's free users get squeezed (StrongLifts, Swipewipe, Timer Plus). Within weeks, several small free or one-time clones appear (Lift5x5, LiftStrong, IntervalFree, dozens of swipe cleaners) and soak up the refugees. If a surge is more than about 4 weeks old, check the clones before validating. The digest's early-surge table could flag surges older than 21 days.
- **Clever Cleaner is a standing kill** for any photo-cleaner cluster (free, ad-free, 205k ratings). It's worth noting in `scout/themes.py` or SCOUT.md so this isn't re-researched.
