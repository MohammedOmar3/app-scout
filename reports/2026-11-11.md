## Notes

- **Tool gap: the targeted collector can't choose storefronts.** `collect.py` searches terms in us and gb only (`--term-storefronts` exists but `collector_ctl.py targeted` doesn't pass it). Apps are scanned in their search storefronts, the storefronts where they chart, and us/gb. So the planned Turkish nl/at/be/fr/ch check was impossible: İslam Vakti came back with us and gb only. Exposing `--term-storefronts` (and a per-app storefront list) in `collector_ctl.py` and the workflow inputs would fix it.
- **Fast followers keep closing windows:** prayer times (Turkish), fasting timers and teleprompters all have a stream of 2026 indie entrants with "no subscription" in the name. Gate B's 4.3 note and the competition score are catching this correctly. Template categories need a wedge that isn't price.
